The problem

Most organizations lose time before they lose trust.

Data quality problems are rarely isolated. A single field mismatch can cascade into payment errors, incomplete claims, delayed reporting, stakeholder confusion, and regulatory risk. The product exists to make that problem visible, explainable, and fixable.

Broken reconciliations

Claims, ledgers, and source records do not always line up, which creates noise in downstream reporting and finance workflows.

Unclear ownership

Teams often know the issue exists but do not know which owner is responsible, which stream is impacted, or what action should happen first.

Manual triage

Quality reviews depend on spreadsheets, scattered notes, and ad hoc follow-up that slows every remediation cycle.

Opaque AI decisions

Teams want recommendations grounded in evidence, not black-box outputs. The platform combines structured data with traceable explanations.

Typical symptoms Duplicate claims, stale reference data, high-risk unmatched volumes, and inconsistent downstream reporting
Operational impact Higher cost to resolve, reduced confidence in metrics, slower decision-making, and increased governance burden
Outcome: organizations need a single quality layer that connects data truth, issue severity, and action ownership.

Core product features

The platform brings together operational intelligence and governance.

Reconciliation engine

Loads source workbooks, standardizes values, compares matched vs unmatched counts, and highlights high-risk fields by stream and report.

Impact and mapping intelligence

Connects each data field to downstream reports, KPIs, and owners so teams can act based on actual business impact rather than raw technical noise.

Multi-domain coverage

How it works

A simple operational flow, from problem to resolution.

1. Ingest
Pulls in source datasets and workbook data from claims, reconciliation files, and BYO inputs without requiring a separate ecosystem of tooling.
2. Standardize
Normalizes fields, maps source-to-target columns, identifies comparable records, and checks for schema drift or type mismatch issues.
3. Score
Applies match-rate, impact score, and risk-tier logic to surface where issues matter most to the business, not just where they appear.
4. Resolve
Generates actions, assigns owners, triggers alerts, and provides dashboards, recommendations, and change guidance across stakeholders.
