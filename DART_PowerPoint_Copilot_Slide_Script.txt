On-slide bullets:

Speaker notes:
Start here by introducing the overall mission and value proposition of DART—the Data Assurance Reconciliation Tracker. Crucially, DART is fully containerized and designed to deploy natively within secure CMS environments without requiring new Authority to Operate (ATO) friction.
